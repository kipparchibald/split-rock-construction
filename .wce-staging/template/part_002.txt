## Article 5. Payment; Draws; Title Protocol

**5.1** All payments are payable only to Split Rock Construction LLC. No field supervisor or subcontractor is authorized to collect Contract funds.

**5.2** **Signing deposit.** At signing of this Agreement, Owner shall pay a **mobilization and permitting deposit of Ten Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($10,000.00)**. That deposit is an advance against the Contract Price (applied to Cost of Work and related P&O for mobilization, permitting, and startup). It is not an additional fee on top of the Contract Price.

**5.3** **Monthly draws after signing.** After the signing deposit, Contractor shall bill **monthly** for Work properly performed and materials suitably stored. Each monthly draw equals: (a) actual Cost of Work incurred to date (and stored materials titled or insured for Owner’s benefit), plus (b) ten percent (10%) P&O on that Cost of Work, less (c) all amounts previously paid (including the $10,000.00 deposit). Owner contingency is **not** advanced on monthly draws unless and until a signed Change Order moves contingency dollars into Cost of Work.

**5.4** Contractor shall submit each monthly draw request with a brief description of Work in place that month and a running reconciliation to the Exhibit B budget target. Owner (or Owner’s title company or lender, if any) shall pay an approved monthly draw within five (5) business days of approval.

**5.5** With each monthly draw after the signing deposit, Contractor shall furnish a conditional waiver of Contractor’s lien rights through the date and amount of that draw. Owner may, at Owner’s reasonable expense, require corresponding subcontractor waivers as disclosed in Exhibit E.

**5.6** Title companies and lenders, if used, shall be told in writing: this file is construction only; do not collect a lot payment; first payment is a $10,000.00 mobilization/permitting deposit at signing; thereafter draws are monthly; do not advance the $29,969.55 contingency except on a signed Change Order.

**5.7** **Final payment.** Final payment is due after certificate of occupancy or equivalent county final, punch-list completion of items Contractor is obligated to complete, delivery of the Idaho Code § 45-525(3) list (Exhibit F), and delivery of final waivers. Unused contingency is credited on final payment. Final payment equals the earned Contract Price (actual Cost of Work + P&O + contingency actually used) less all prior payments.

**5.8** Idaho Code § 29-115 limits retainage to five percent (5%) on most private construction contracts, but that statutory cap does not apply to a private work of improvement to residential real property consisting of one to four units occupied or to be occupied by the owner. The parties elect the signing-deposit-plus-monthly-draw method in Exhibit C in lieu of a separate retainage holdback, except that Owner may withhold one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the reasonable value of unfinished punch-list work or a bona fide dispute until resolved.
