## Article 7. Delays, Concealed Conditions, and Force Majeure

**7.1** If Contractor encounters subsurface or otherwise concealed physical conditions that differ materially from those indicated in the Plans or from those ordinarily found in Jefferson County residential work, Contractor shall stop the affected Work and give Owner written notice within three (3) business days. The parties shall price the change before the affected Work resumes, except for an emergency protecting persons or the Work.

**7.2** Neither party is liable for delay caused by fire, flood, earthquake, epidemic, war, government order, utility failure, or comparable force majeure. Contractor’s sole remedy is an extension of time and, if the event increases Cost of Work, a Change Order against contingency first.

## Article 8. Idaho Code § 45-525 Disclosures

**8.1** Before signing this Agreement, Owner received and signed the Idaho Code § 45-525(2) initial disclosure attached as Exhibit E. Contractor shall retain the signed receipt and give Owner a copy.

**8.2** Before final payment, Contractor shall deliver the Idaho Code § 45-525(3) written list of subcontractors, materialmen, and rental-equipment providers having a direct contract with Contractor who supplied more than $500 of work or materials. Exhibit F is that form. It is blank until that time.

## Article 9. Change Orders and Contingency

**9.1** No change in price, scope, or schedule is binding unless both parties sign a Change Order in the form of Exhibit D, except that documented emergency work may start immediately if a Change Order is signed within forty-eight (48) hours.

**9.2** The Owner contingency of $29,969.55 is reserved for unknown site conditions, weather protection reasonably required to protect the Work, and code or inspector-directed items not shown on the Plans. It is not profit, not an upgrade budget, and not a silent fund for Owner upgrades.

**9.3** Contingency moves only on a signed Change Order. Unused contingency credits final payment. If contingency is used, profit and overhead of ten percent (10%) applies only to the contingency dollars actually used.

## Article 10. Owner’s Responsibilities

**10.1** Owner shall provide continuous access to the Property, timely selections, timely draw payments, and decisions within five (5) business days of a written request.

**10.2** Owner shall not issue directions to subcontractors. All instructions go through Contractor.

**10.3** Owner shall carry insurance on Owner’s personal property stored at the site. Owner shall disclose any lender or title-draw requirements before the signing deposit.
