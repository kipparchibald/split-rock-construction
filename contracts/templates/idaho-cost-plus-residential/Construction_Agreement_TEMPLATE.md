## Article 5. Payment; Draws; Title Protocol

**5.1** All payments are payable only to Split Rock Construction LLC. No field supervisor or subcontractor is authorized to collect Contract funds.

**5.2** **Signing deposit.** At signing of this Agreement, Owner shall pay a **mobilization and permitting deposit of Ten Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($10,000.00)**. That deposit is an advance against the Contract Price (applied to Cost of Work and related P&O for mobilization, permitting, and startup). It is not an additional fee on top of the Contract Price.

**5.3** **Monthly draws after signing.** After the signing deposit, Contractor shall bill **monthly** for Work properly performed and materials suitably stored. Each monthly draw equals: (a) actual Cost of Work incurred to date (and stored materials titled or insured for Owner’s benefit), plus (b) ten percent (10%) P&O on that Cost of Work, less (c) all amounts previously paid (including the $10,000.00 deposit). Owner contingency is **not** advanced on monthly draws unless and until a signed Change Order moves contingency dollars into Cost of Work.

**5.4** Contractor shall submit each monthly draw request with a brief description of Work in place that month and a running reconciliation to the Exhibit B budget target. Owner (or Owner’s title company or lender, if any) shall pay an approved monthly draw within five (5) business days of approval.

**5.5** With each monthly draw after the signing deposit, Contractor shall furnish a conditional waiver of Contractor’s lien rights through the date and amount of that draw. Owner may, at Owner’s reasonable expense, require corresponding subcontractor waivers as disclosed in Exhibit E.

**5.6** Title companies and lenders, if used, shall be told in writing: this file is construction only; do not collect a lot payment; first payment is a $10,000.00 mobilization/permitting deposit at signing; thereafter draws are monthly; do not advance the $29,969.55 contingency except on a signed Change Order.

**5.7** **Final payment.** Final payment is due after certificate of occupancy or equivalent county final, punch-list completion of items Contractor is obligated to complete, delivery of the Idaho Code § 45-525(3) list (Exhibit F), and delivery of final waivers. Unused contingency is credited on final payment. Final payment equals the earned Contract Price (actual Cost of Work + P&O + contingency actually used) less all prior payments.

**5.8** Idaho Code § 29-115 limits retainage to five percent (5%) on most private construction contracts, but that statutory cap does not apply to a private work of improvement to residential real property consisting of one to four units occupied or to be occupied by the owner. The parties elect the signing-deposit-plus-monthly-draw method in Exhibit C in lieu of a separate retainage holdback, except that Owner may withhold one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the reasonable value of unfinished punch-list work or a bona fide dispute until resolved.

## Article 14. Default and Termination; Mutual Termination

**14.1** If Owner fails to pay an undisputed draw within ten (10) days after written notice, Contractor may suspend the Work and recover demobilization and remobilization costs as a Change Order. If nonpayment continues thirty (30) days after that notice, Contractor may terminate and recover the unpaid earned portion of the Contract Price, reasonable demobilization, and profit on Work performed.

**14.2** If Contractor fails to prosecute the Work with reasonable diligence or otherwise materially defaults, Owner shall give written notice. If Contractor does not commence a cure within fourteen (14) days and complete the cure within a reasonable time, Owner may terminate for default and finish the Work by other means. Owner’s damages are limited to the reasonable extra cost to complete the remaining Work, less unpaid Contract balance.

**14.3** Either party may terminate for force majeure that continues more than sixty (60) consecutive days. In that event Contractor is paid for Work properly performed and for materials stored and titled to Owner.

**14.4** Mutual termination. The parties may terminate this Agreement at any time by a writing signed by Owner and Contractor that states the termination date and the settlement amount. On a mutual termination: (a) Contractor shall stop the Work, make the Property reasonably safe and weathertight, and demobilize; (b) Owner shall pay Contractor, within ten (10) days of an itemized invoice, the earned unpaid portion of the Contract Price for Work properly performed, plus the actual unrecoverable cost of materials and equipment already ordered that cannot be cancelled or restocked, plus reasonable demobilization, less any amounts Owner has already paid; (c) unused Owner Contingency is not earned and is not paid; (d) Contractor is not entitled to profit or overhead on Work not performed; (e) Contractor shall deliver to Owner all plans, permits in process, lien waivers for Work paid, and materials stored and titled to Owner; and (f) except for payment of the settlement, warranty on Work performed, indemnity for Work performed, and confidentiality, both parties are released from further performance. Mutual termination is not a default by either party and shall not be described as such to a lender, the county, or a third party.

**14.5** Termination for convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by written notice delivered at least fourteen (14) days before the effective date. The settlement is the same as Section 14.4(a)–(f). A convenience termination is not a default. If Owner terminates for convenience after Contractor has ordered long-lead items in good faith, those items are part of the settlement under 14.4(b). If Contractor terminates for convenience, Contractor shall cooperate in an orderly handoff to Owner or a successor contractor and shall not remove materials already paid for by Owner.
