## Why this paper exists

Idaho Code § 54-2055 requires an active licensee who is buying, selling, or otherwise acquiring or disposing of an interest in real property in a regulated real estate transaction to disclose in writing that the person holds an active Idaho real estate license, and to conduct personal real-estate transactions through the broker with whom the licensee is licensed.

This construction contract is **not** a purchase and sale of the lot. Best practice — and Don’s file standard — is still a written dual-capacity disclosure because:

- the same licensee sold the Owners the lot and now proposes to build on it;
- the licensee has a financial interest in the construction company that will be paid from draws;
- IREC expects the responsible broker to know about related transactions involving an associated licensee.

Idaho Code § 54-2085 required the IREC Agency Disclosure Brochure at first substantial contact on the **lot** transaction. That receipt should already be in the Alliance / brokerage lot file. This paper does not replace that brochure.
