## 1. PROPERTY
Lot 16, Block 8, Teton Heights Division 6, Jefferson County, Idaho ("Property"). Legal description as recorded: NNA Bareground, Rigby, ID 83442. Address to be assigned upon platting.

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor shall construct a single-family residence on the Property in substantial accordance with the plans and specifications prepared by River Bend Drafting and Design (Elise Shurtliff), dated August 31, 2026, including all sheets P-1 through P-8 (the "Plans"), and the Key Specifications summarized in Exhibit A attached hereto (collectively, the "Contract Documents").

The Work includes, without limitation:
- Site preparation, excavation, and grading as shown on the Plot Plan (P-8).
- Foundation system: 12" diameter concrete piers with (6) #4 vertical rebar over 24"x24"x10" footings with (3) #4 E.W. over undisturbed soil; concrete stem walls per Plan P-4; 4' crawlspace.
- Framing: 2x6 exterior walls @ 24" O.C. (UNO), 2x4 interior walls @ 24" O.C., engineered floor joists (11-7/8" TJI), 6:12 pitch roof with engineered trusses @ 24" O.C.
- Exterior: vinyl siding and brick veneer over weather-resistive barrier and 7/16" OSB; 25-year architectural asphalt shingles over #15 felt and ice barrier; 3/8" soffit with continuous venting.
- Interior: 1/2" gypsum board (5/8" Type X over garage), taped and prepped for finishes; R-49 blown attic insulation; R-21 wall insulation; R-30 floor insulation.
- Garage: 4" concrete slab with reinforcing wire fabric; zero-entry into house.
- Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing rough-in and finish per 2018 IRC and local code.
- Septic system and replacement area approximately 20' x 60' as shown on Plot Plan.
- Patios (front and back) as shown.

Exclusions (unless otherwise agreed in writing): landscaping, fencing, appliances (unless specified), window coverings, and any items not shown on the Plans.

## 4. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Payments shall be made by Owner to Contractor according to the following schedule, tied to milestones. Each progress payment is due within five (5) business days of Contractor's written invoice and Owner's reasonable verification that the milestone has been achieved.

| Milestone | Amount | Cumulative |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| 1. Upon execution of this Agreement and commencement of site work | $10,000.00 | $10,000.00 |
| 2. Foundation complete (piers, footings, stem walls, backfill) | $15,000.00 | $25,000.00 |
| 3. Framing, sheathing, and roof dried-in | $20,000.00 | $45,000.00 |
| 4. Exterior complete (siding, brick, windows, doors, roofing) | $15,000.00 | $60,000.00 |
| 5. Rough-in complete (MEP, insulation) | $15,000.00 | $75,000.00 |
| 6. Interior finishes substantially complete | $15,000.00 | $90,000.00 |
| 7. Final payment upon Certificate of Occupancy and punch-list completion | $9,500.00 | $99,500.00 |

Retainage: Owner may withhold up to five percent (5%) of each progress payment as retainage, not to exceed five percent (5%) of the Contract Price, in accordance with Idaho Code § 29-115. Retainage shall be released within thirty-five (35) days of final completion, subject to satisfactory lien waivers.

No payment shall be due for work not yet performed or materials not yet delivered to the site.

## 5. TIME FOR PERFORMANCE
Contractor shall commence the Work within fourteen (14) days after (a) receipt of all required permits, (b) Owner's delivery of the lot in buildable condition, and (c) the initial payment. Estimated substantial completion: approximately nine (9) to twelve (12) months from commencement, subject to weather, permit delays, material availability, and other excusable delays.

Contractor shall notify Owner in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of any event reasonably expected to delay the project. Excusable delays include acts of God, weather, permit delays, material shortages, labor disputes, and government orders. Time is not of the essence except as expressly stated.

## 6. CHANGE ORDERS
Any change to the scope, price, or schedule must be documented in a written Change Order signed by both parties before the changed work begins. Each Change Order shall describe the change, the adjustment to the Contract Price (if any), and the adjustment to the schedule (if any). Verbal changes are not binding.

## 7. PERMITS AND CODES
Contractor shall obtain and pay for all building permits and inspections required for the Work. The Work shall comply with the 2018 International Residential Code (IRC) as adopted by Jefferson County, Idaho, and all applicable local amendments. Plans are for code compliance reference; any local code or engineer/architect specification supersedes the Plans.

## 8. INSURANCE AND BONDING
Contractor shall maintain general liability insurance including completed operations and workers' compensation insurance as required by Idaho law. Owner has the right to receive proof of such coverage upon request (see Initial Disclosure, Idaho Code § 45-525(2)).

Owner has the right, at Owner's expense, to require a surety bond in an amount up to the value of the project.

## 9. LIEN WAIVERS
Owner has the right, at Owner's reasonable expense, to require Contractor to obtain lien waivers from subcontractors and materialmen (see Initial Disclosure).

## 10. SUBCONTRACTORS
Contractor may engage subcontractors and materialmen as Contractor deems appropriate. Prior to final payment or closing, Contractor shall provide the Completion Disclosure listing all subcontractors, materialmen, and rental equipment providers with direct contracts exceeding $500 (Idaho Code § 45-525(3)).

## 11. WARRANTY
Contractor warrants that the Work will be free from defects in workmanship and materials for a period of one (1) year from the date of substantial completion, or longer as required by applicable law or manufacturer warranties, which Contractor shall pass through to Owner. Contractor shall repair or replace defective Work within a reasonable time after written notice.

This warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, Owner-caused damage, or items outside Contractor's control.

## 12. INSPECTION AND PUNCH LIST
Owner shall have the right to inspect the Work at reasonable times. Upon substantial completion, Contractor shall prepare a punch list of incomplete or defective items. Final payment is contingent upon completion of the punch list to Owner's reasonable satisfaction and issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy.

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to non-binding mediation in Jefferson County, Idaho. If still unresolved, either party may pursue remedies in the state courts of Jefferson County, Idaho. Each party shall bear its own costs unless otherwise awarded by the court.

## 14. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the breach remains uncured. Owner may terminate for convenience upon written notice, in which case Contractor shall be paid for Work performed and materials ordered to date, plus reasonable overhead and profit on the terminated portion.

## 15. MISCELLANEOUS
This Agreement, together with the Plans, Exhibit A (Key Specifications), the Initial Disclosure, and any Change Orders, constitutes the entire agreement. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. Governing law: State of Idaho.

## EXHIBIT A — KEY SPECIFICATIONS (from River Bend Drafting Plans, 8/31/2026)

**General:**
- Climate Zone 6 insulation: Ceiling R-49, Walls R-20+5/13+10, Floors R-30, Basement R-15/20.
- Concrete: 2,500 PSI (not exposed to weather); 3,000 PSI (exposed to weather).
- Soil bearing: 2,000 PSF assumed; contractor to verify.
- Footings: 16" wide x 8" deep (6" or 8" wall) or 20" wide x 10" deep (8' wall), with (2) #4 rebar.
- Vapor retarder: Class I or II on interior side of frame walls.
- Ice barrier: 24" min. from eave edge inside exterior wall line.
- Garage separation: 1/2" gypsum (5/8" Type X above garage).
- Grade away from foundation: 6" in first 10 feet.

**Structure:**
- Foundation: 12" dia. concrete piers w/ (6) #4 vert. rebar over 24"x24"x10" footings w/ (3) #4 E.W.
- Crawlspace: 4' depth; foundation vents per R408; 18"x24" access.
- Exterior walls: 2x6 studs @ 24" O.C. (UNO), (2) 2x6 top plates, 1x6 bottom plate, R-21 batt, 1/2" sheetrock inside.
- Interior walls: 2x4 studs @ 24" O.C., (2) 2x4 top plates, 1x4 bottom plate, 1/2" sheetrock both sides.
- Floor: 3/4" T&G subfloor glued/nailed over 11-7/8" TJI engineered joists per layout.
- Roof: 6:12 pitch; engineered trusses @ 24" O.C.; 7/16" OSB sheathing; #15 felt; 25-yr architectural shingles; R-49 blown insulation; 5/8" sheetrock ceiling.
- Attic access: 22"x30" (contractor to locate if not specified).
- Soffit: 2x6 fascia, style D metal drip edge, 3/8" soffit, 3" continuous venting.

**Exterior:**
- Vinyl siding and brick over WRB and 7/16" OSB (nail 6" edge, 12" field).
- Decks (if any): 2x10 treated ledger, rim, joists @ 16" O.C., 2x decking; railings per code.

**MEP & Safety:**
- Electrical: AFCI on bedroom 125V circuits; meter base per utility; main panel in mechanical room.
- Smoke alarms: UL 217, interconnected, battery backup — one per sleeping area, outside each, each story.
- CO alarms: UL 2034, outside each sleeping area.
- Egress windows: 5.7 sq ft min opening, sill ≤ 44"; wells 9 sq ft net, 36" projection/width; ladder if >44" deep.
- Ventilation: whole house 80 CFM, kitchen 100, laundry 50, baths 50, bedrooms/living 10 CFM fresh air.
- Stairs: 7-3/4" max riser, 10" min tread, 3 stringers, 36" handrail, 42" guards, 4" sphere rule.
- Guards: 36" min height, no ladder effect, 4" sphere rule.

**Plot:**
- Overall: 77'-6" x 57'-1" (approx.); garage 29'-8" x 37'-11"; main floor 30'-0" x 38'-0".
- Septic + replacement area: approx. 20' x 60'.
- Driveway, well, and utilities as shown on P-8.
