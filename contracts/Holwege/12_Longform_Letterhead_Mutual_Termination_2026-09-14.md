Residential New Construction — State of Idaho

This Construction Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of _________________, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), by and between:

CONTRACTOR: Split Rock Construction LLC, an Idaho limited liability company, Idaho contractor registration 6481622, 527 Rigby Town Square, Rigby, Idaho 83442; telephone (208) 200-0605; email Kipp@splitrockconst.com (“Contractor”).

OWNER: Lauren Holwege and Cindy Holwege, jointly and severally (“Owner”).

Contractor and Owner are the only parties to this Agreement. Any field supervisor, superintendent, or subcontractor is not a party and has no authority to change price, scope, schedule, or payment terms.

Archibald-Bagley Real Estate and its designated broker and agents are not parties to this Agreement and do not represent Owner in connection with this construction work. This Agreement is a construction contract. It is not a regulated real estate transaction, listing, purchase-and-sale agreement, or brokerage representation agreement.

In consideration of the mutual covenants below, the parties agree as follows.

## Article 1. Property

**1.1** The Work will be performed on Lot 16, Block 8, Teton Heights Division 6, Jefferson County, Idaho, as described in Deed Instrument No. 501245 (the “Property”).

**1.2** Owner represents that Owner already owns the Property in fee simple, free of any construction-contract claim by Contractor against the lot purchase. This Agreement is for construction of the residence on that Property only. No lot purchase price is included in the Contract Price. Title companies and lenders shall not collect a lot payment under this Agreement.

## Article 2. The Work and Contract Documents

**2.1** Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, supervision, and services reasonably required to construct the residence shown on the River Bend Drafting and Design plans dated 31 August 2026, file name “Lauren and Cindy Holwege 8-31-2026b,” prepared by Elise Shurtliff (the “Plans”), as summarized in Exhibit A and priced in Exhibit B.

**2.2** The Contract Documents are this Agreement, the Plans, Exhibit A (Key Specifications), Exhibit B (Cost of Work), Exhibit C (Draw Schedule), Exhibit D (Change Order form), the signed Idaho Code § 45-525(2) Initial Disclosure attached as Exhibit E, and all written Change Orders. If documents conflict, this Agreement controls over exhibits, and the Plans control over the specification summary except as to exclusions and allowances stated here.

**2.3** Already at the Property: natural gas service, electrical service with transformer set, and fiber. Remaining utility work under this Agreement is house laterals, meter bases, and yard lines only. Contractor shall not install a propane tank, a new transformer, or a primary power or gas main extension unless the parties sign a Change Order.

**2.4** Excluded from the Work unless added by Change Order: landscaping, sod, irrigation, fencing, mailbox, window coverings, a propane tank and first fill, a new transformer or primary utility run, and any line marked Excluded on Exhibit B.

**2.5** Work shall comply with the issued building permit and applicable codes of Jefferson County and the State of Idaho. Contractor shall post its contractor registration number at the jobsite as required by Idaho Code § 54-5209.

**2.6** Labor on this job will be performed by independent, Idaho-registered subcontractors under contract with Contractor. Contractor currently has no employees. Contractor shall engage only Idaho-registered contractors where registration is required (Idaho Code § 54-5204).

## Article 5. Payment; Draws; Title Protocol

**5.1** All payments are payable only to Split Rock Construction LLC. No field supervisor or subcontractor is authorized to collect Contract funds.

**5.2** The draw base for Draws 1 through 5 is $659,330.10 (Cost of Work plus profit and overhead). Owner contingency is not advanced on Draws 1 through 5.

**5.3** Draws are as set out in Exhibit C. Contractor shall submit a draw request describing the milestone reached. Owner (or Owner’s title company or lender, if any) shall pay an approved draw within five (5) business days of approval.

**5.4** With each draw after Draw 1, Contractor shall furnish a conditional waiver of Contractor’s lien rights through the date and amount of that draw. Owner may, at Owner’s reasonable expense, require corresponding subcontractor waivers as disclosed in Exhibit E.

**5.5** Title companies and lenders, if used, shall be told in writing: this file is construction only; do not collect a lot payment; do not advance the $29,969.55 contingency on Draws 1–5.

**5.6** Final payment (Draw 6) is due after certificate of occupancy or equivalent county final, punch-list completion of items Contractor is obligated to complete, delivery of the Idaho Code § 45-525(3) list (Exhibit F), and delivery of final waivers. Unused contingency is credited on Draw 6.

**5.7** Idaho Code § 29-115 limits retainage to five percent (5%) on most private construction contracts, but that statutory cap does not apply to a private work of improvement to residential real property consisting of one to four units occupied or to be occupied by the owner. The parties elect the milestone draw schedule in Exhibit C in lieu of a separate retainage holdback, except that Owner may withhold one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the reasonable value of unfinished punch-list work or a bona fide dispute until resolved.

## Article 6. Time

**6.1** Contractor shall start the Work within fourteen (14) days after the later of: (a) the Effective Date; (b) issuance of the building permit; and (c) Owner’s payment of Draw 1.

**6.2** Substantial Completion target: _________________ (to be filled before signing). “Substantial Completion” means the residence is sufficiently complete that Owner can occupy it for its intended use, subject only to punch-list items that do not prevent occupancy, and a certificate of occupancy or equivalent county final has been issued or is obtainable upon completion of those items.

**6.3** Time is important to both parties. The Substantial Completion date shall be extended day-for-day for Owner delay, selections delay, Change Order work, concealed conditions, weather that reasonably prevents the then-critical activity, labor or material unavailability not caused by Contractor, permit or inspection delay not caused by Contractor, and other events beyond Contractor’s reasonable control.

## Article 7. Delays, Concealed Conditions, and Force Majeure

**7.1** If Contractor encounters subsurface or otherwise concealed physical conditions that differ materially from those indicated in the Plans or from those ordinarily found in Jefferson County residential work, Contractor shall stop the affected Work and give Owner written notice within three (3) business days. The parties shall price the change before the affected Work resumes, except for an emergency protecting persons or the Work.

**7.2** Neither party is liable for delay caused by fire, flood, earthquake, epidemic, war, government order, utility failure, or comparable force majeure. Contractor’s sole remedy is an extension of time and, if the event increases Cost of Work, a Change Order against contingency first.

## Article 8. Idaho Code § 45-525 Disclosures

**8.1** Before signing this Agreement, Owner received and signed the Idaho Code § 45-525(2) initial disclosure attached as Exhibit E. Contractor shall retain the signed receipt and give Owner a copy.

**8.2** Before Draw 6 or final payment, Contractor shall deliver the Idaho Code § 45-525(3) written list of subcontractors, materialmen, and rental-equipment providers having a direct contract with Contractor who supplied more than $500 of work or materials. Exhibit F is that form. It is blank until that time.

**8.3** As required by § 45-525(2), Owner is informed of Owner’s rights, at Owner’s expense, to require lien waivers from subcontractors, to receive proof of Contractor’s general-liability insurance including completed operations and worker’s compensation for employees as required by Idaho law, to purchase an extended title policy covering certain unfiled liens, and to require a surety bond up to the value of the project.

## Article 9. Change Orders and Contingency

**9.1** No change in price, scope, or schedule is binding unless both parties sign a Change Order in the form of Exhibit D, except that documented emergency work may start immediately if a Change Order is signed within forty-eight (48) hours.

**9.2** The Owner contingency of $29,969.55 is reserved for unknown site conditions, weather protection reasonably required to protect the Work, and code or inspector-directed items not shown on the Plans. It is not profit, not an upgrade budget, and not a fund to cover a Bid miss.

**9.3** Contingency moves only on a signed Change Order. Unused contingency credits Draw 6. If contingency is used, profit and overhead of ten percent (10%) applies only to the contingency dollars actually used.

## Article 10. Owner’s Responsibilities

**10.1** Owner shall provide continuous access to the Property, timely selections, timely draw payments, and decisions within five (5) business days of a written request.

**10.2** Owner shall not issue directions to subcontractors. All instructions go through Contractor.

**10.3** Owner shall carry insurance on Owner’s personal property stored at the site. Owner shall disclose any lender or title-draw requirements before Draw 1.

## Article 11. Site, Safety, and Owner Occupancy During Construction

**11.1** The site is a construction area. Owner, guests, and agents enter at their own risk and shall follow Contractor’s safety directions. Children shall not be on site unsupervised.

**11.2** Owner shall not occupy the residence overnight before Substantial Completion without Contractor’s written consent, which may be conditioned on insurance and punch-list terms.

## Article 12. Substantial Completion, Punch List, and Final Completion

**12.1** When Contractor considers the Work substantially complete, Contractor shall notify Owner and schedule a walk-through. The parties shall list remaining punch items. Contractor shall complete those items with reasonable diligence.

**12.2** Final Completion occurs when punch-list work Contractor is obligated to perform is done, the § 45-525(3) list is delivered, final waivers are delivered, and Draw 6 is paid.

## Article 13. Warranty

**13.1** Contractor warrants that the Work will be performed in a workmanlike manner and will conform to the Contract Documents and applicable code for a period of one (1) year after Substantial Completion (the “Warranty Period”).

**13.2** Manufacturer and supplier warranties on equipment, appliances, windows, roofing, and similar products are assigned to Owner at Final Completion to the extent assignable. Contractor’s obligation on those items after installation is to assist Owner in making a warranty claim, not to expand the manufacturer’s warranty.

**13.3** The warranty does not cover Owner maintenance, abuse, normal wear, Owner-supplied materials, work by others, or movement or drainage issues caused by Owner’s later landscaping.

**13.4** Owner shall give written notice of a warranty item during the Warranty Period. Contractor shall begin repair of covered items within a reasonable time after notice, weather and access permitting.

## Article 14. Default and Termination; Mutual Termination

**14.1** If Owner fails to pay an undisputed draw within ten (10) days after written notice, Contractor may suspend the Work and recover demobilization and remobilization costs as a Change Order. If nonpayment continues thirty (30) days after that notice, Contractor may terminate and recover the unpaid earned portion of the Contract Price, reasonable demobilization, and profit on Work performed.

**14.2** If Contractor fails to prosecute the Work with reasonable diligence or otherwise materially defaults, Owner shall give written notice. If Contractor does not commence a cure within fourteen (14) days and complete the cure within a reasonable time, Owner may terminate for default and finish the Work by other means. Owner’s damages are limited to the reasonable extra cost to complete the remaining Work, less unpaid Contract balance.
