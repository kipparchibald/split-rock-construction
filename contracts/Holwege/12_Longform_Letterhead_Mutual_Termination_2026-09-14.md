**9.3** Contingency moves only on a signed Change Order. Unused contingency credits Draw 6. If contingency is used, profit and overhead of ten percent (10%) applies only to the contingency dollars actually used.

**11.2** Owner shall not occupy the residence overnight before Substantial Completion without Contractor’s written consent, which may be conditioned on insurance and punch-list terms.

## Article 12. Substantial Completion, Punch List, and Final Completion

**12.1** When Contractor considers the Work substantially complete, Contractor shall notify Owner and schedule a walk-through. The parties shall list remaining punch items. Contractor shall complete those items with reasonable diligence.

**12.2** Final Completion occurs when punch-list work Contractor is obligated to perform is done, the § 45-525(3) list is delivered, final waivers are delivered, and Draw 6 is paid.

## Article 13. Warranty

**13.1** Contractor warrants that the Work will be performed in a workmanlike manner and will conform to the Contract Documents and applicable code for a period of one (1) year after Substantial Completion (the “Warranty Period”).

**13.2** Manufacturer and supplier warranties on equipment, appliances, windows, roofing, and similar products are assigned to Owner at Final Completion to the extent assignable. Contractor’s obligation on those items after installation is to assist Owner in making a warranty claim, not to expand the manufacturer’s warranty.

**13.3** The warranty does not cover Owner maintenance, abuse, normal wear, Owner-supplied materials, work by others, or movement or drainage issues caused by Owner’s later landscaping.

**13.4** Owner shall give written notice of a warranty item during the Warranty Period. Contractor shall begin repair of covered items within a reasonable time after notice, weather and access permitting.

## Article 14. Default and Termination; Mutual Termination

**14.1** If Owner fails to pay an undisputed draw within ten (10) days after written notice, Contractor may suspend the Work and recover demobilization and remobilization costs as a Change Order. If nonpayment continues thirty (30) days after that notice, Contractor may terminate and recover the unpaid earned portion of the Contract Price, reasonable demobilization, and profit on Work performed.

**14.2** If Contractor fails to prosecute the Work with reasonable diligence or otherwise materially defaults, Owner shall give written notice. If Contractor does not commence a cure within fourteen (14) days and complete the cure within a reasonable time, Owner may terminate for default and finish the Work by other means. Owner’s damages are limited to the reasonable extra cost to complete the remaining Work, less unpaid Contract balance.

**14.3** Either party may terminate for force majeure that continues more than sixty (60) consecutive days. In that event Contractor is paid for Work properly performed and for materials stored and titled to Owner.

**14.4** Mutual termination. The parties may terminate this Agreement at any time by a writing signed by Owner and Contractor that states the termination date and the settlement amount. On a mutual termination: (a) Contractor shall stop the Work, make the Property reasonably safe and weathertight, and demobilize; (b) Owner shall pay Contractor, within ten (10) days of an itemized invoice, the earned unpaid portion of the Contract Price for Work properly performed, plus the actual unrecoverable cost of materials and equipment already ordered that cannot be cancelled or restocked, plus reasonable demobilization, less any amounts Owner has already paid; (c) unused Owner Contingency is not earned and is not paid; (d) Contractor is not entitled to profit or overhead on Work not performed; (e) Contractor shall deliver to Owner all plans, permits in process, lien waivers for Work paid, and materials stored and titled to Owner; and (f) except for payment of the settlement, warranty on Work performed, indemnity for Work performed, and confidentiality, both parties are released from further performance. Mutual termination is not a default by either party and shall not be described as such to a lender, the county, or a third party.

**14.5** Termination for convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by written notice delivered at least fourteen (14) days before the effective date. The settlement is the same as Section 14.4(a)–(f). A convenience termination is not a default. If Owner terminates for convenience after Contractor has ordered long-lead items in good faith, those items are part of the settlement under 14.4(b). If Contractor terminates for convenience, Contractor shall cooperate in an orderly handoff to Owner or a successor contractor and shall not remove materials already paid for by Owner.

## Article 15. Insurance

**15.1** Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance including products and completed operations with a per-occurrence limit of not less than Three Hundred Thousand Dollars ($300,000), which is the statutory minimum associated with Idaho contractor registration (Idaho Code § 54-5210). Upon request Contractor shall furnish a certificate naming Owner as certificate holder.

**15.2** Contractor currently has no employees. Working members of an LLC are not required to carry worker’s compensation on themselves. Idaho requires worker’s compensation when Contractor has employees. Contractor therefore has no worker’s compensation certificate to deliver at signing. If Contractor later hires any employee, Contractor shall obtain worker’s compensation before that person starts work and shall furnish the certificate on request.

**15.3** Contractor shall require each subcontractor to carry its own worker’s compensation or a lawful exemption, and general liability appropriate to the trade.

**15.4** Builder’s risk / course-of-construction insurance in an amount not less than the Contract Price shall be maintained from mobilization through Substantial Completion. Unless Owner’s lender requires Owner to purchase it, Contractor shall purchase it (budgeted as the Exhibit B builders-risk line) and shall name Owner as loss payee as their interests appear. Deductible shall not exceed $2,500 without Owner’s written consent.

## Article 16. Indemnification (Idaho Code § 29-114)

**16.1** Contractor shall indemnify and hold Owner harmless from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by the negligent acts or omissions of Contractor or its subcontractors in performing the Work.

**16.2** Owner shall indemnify and hold Contractor harmless from third-party claims to the extent caused by Owner’s negligent acts or omissions, including site visitors Owner invites.

**16.3** Idaho Code § 29-114 voids any construction-contract clause that requires a party to indemnify another person for loss caused by the indemnitee’s sole negligence. Nothing in this Agreement requires that indemnity. Each party’s indemnity is limited to its own proportionate fault.

## Article 17. Liens

**17.1** Contractor shall keep the Property free of liens arising from Contractor’s failure to pay subcontractors or suppliers for Work for which Owner has paid Contractor, provided Owner is not in payment default.

**17.2** Owner acknowledges that unpaid subcontractors and suppliers may have lien rights against the Property even if Owner has paid Contractor, which is why § 45-525 exists and why Owner may require waivers at Owner’s expense.

## Article 18. Dispute Resolution

**18.1** The parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation for ten (10) days after written notice of a dispute.

**18.2** If negotiation fails, either party may require non-binding mediation in Jefferson County, Idaho, before a mutually chosen mediator. Mediation fees are split equally.

**18.3** If mediation does not resolve the dispute within forty-five (45) days after the mediation demand, either party may file in the state courts of Idaho. Exclusive venue is Jefferson County, Idaho. Idaho law governs.

**18.4** The prevailing party in any action to enforce this Agreement is entitled to reasonable attorney fees and costs as allowed by Idaho law.

## Article 19. Home Solicitation Cancellation

**19.1** If Owner signs this Agreement at a place other than Contractor’s regular place of business (including Owner’s current residence), Idaho Code §§ 28-43-401 to 28-43-403 and the FTC Cooling-Off Rule may give Owner the right to cancel until midnight of the third business day after signing.
