# KEY SPECIFICATIONS SUMMARY

## Dimensions & Layout
- Overall footprint: approximately 77'-6" x 57'-1"
- Garage: 29'-8" x 37'-11" (zero entry into house)
- Main floor: 30'-0" x 38'-0"
- Main walls: 9' height
- Roof pitch: 6:12 (main, front, sides); some 8:12 and 3:12 accents
- Crawlspace: 4' depth
- Front and back patios

## Foundation
- 12" diameter concrete piers with (6) #4 vertical rebar over 24"x24"x10" footings with (3) #4 E.W. over undisturbed soil
- Concrete stem walls (8' and under: 1 horizontal rebar top 12" + mid-height; 8'1"–9'6": rebar at thirds)
- 2x6 treated sill plate over foam sill sealer; 1/2" anchor bolts @ 72" O.C., 10" long, 7" embed
- Asphalt emulsion on exterior of foundation wall
- 4" concrete slab in garage with reinforcing wire fabric
- Concrete strength: 2,500 PSI (not exposed); 3,000 PSI (exposed to weather)
- Footings: 16" wide x 8" deep (for 6"/8" walls) or 20" wide x 10" deep (8' walls), (2) #4 rebar; soil bearing 2,000 PSF assumed

## Framing
- Exterior walls: 2x6 studs @ 24" O.C. (UNO), (2) 2x6 top plates, 1x6 bottom plate, R-21 batt insulation, 1/2" sheetrock inside
- Interior walls: 2x4 studs @ 24" O.C., (2) 2x4 top plates, 1x4 bottom plate, 1/2" sheetrock both sides
- Floor: 3/4" T&G subfloor glued and nailed over 11-7/8" TJI engineered joists (layout per TJXpert)
- Roof: 6:12 pitch engineered trusses @ 24" O.C.; 7/16" OSB sheathing (nail 6" edge, 10" field); #15 building felt; 25-year architectural asphalt shingles; R-49 blown attic insulation; 5/8" sheetrock ceiling
- Attic access: 22"x30" (contractor to locate)
- Soffit: 2x6 fascia, style D metal drip edge, 3/8" soffit, 3" continuous venting

## Exterior Finishes
- Vinyl siding and brick veneer over weather-resistive barrier and 7/16" OSB
- Ice barrier: 2 layers cemented underlayment or self-adhering polymer-modified bitumen, extending 24" min. inside exterior wall line from eave
- Decks (if included): 2x10 treated ledger, rim, joists @ 16" O.C., 2x decking; railings per code

## Insulation (Climate Zone 6)
- Ceiling: R-49
- Walls: R-20 + 5/13 + 10
- Floors: R-30
- Basement/crawl: R-15/20

## MEP & Safety
- Electrical: AFCI protection on bedroom 125V 15/20A circuits; meter base per utility; main panel in mechanical room
- Smoke alarms: UL 217, interconnected with battery backup — one per sleeping area, outside each, each story
- CO alarms: UL 2034, outside each sleeping area
- Egress windows: 5.7 sq ft min opening, sill ≤ 44"; wells 9 sq ft net clear, 36" projection/width; ladder if depth >44"
- Ventilation: whole house 80 CFM, kitchen 100, laundry 50, baths 50, bedrooms/living 10 CFM fresh air
- Stairs: 7-3/4" max riser, 10" min tread, 3 stringers, 36" handrail, 42" guards, 4" sphere rule
- Guards: 36" min, no ladder effect
- Vapor retarder: Class I or II on interior of frame walls

- Septic system + replacement area: approx. 20' x 60'
- Driveway, well, and utilities as shown
- Grade away from foundation: 6" in first 10 feet
