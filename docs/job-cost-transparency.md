# Job-cost transparency and trade secrets

## What we share with the owner
- Budgeted vs. actual totals per line item
- Running variance and the 50/50 shared-savings math
- Draw schedule and deposit status
- Names of subcontractors and material suppliers (required by Idaho Code 45-525)

## What stays private
- Individual competing sub bids and supplier quotes
- Subcontractor labor rates, material breakdowns, and internal margins
- Our negotiated relationships and which subs we prefer
- Any internal estimating assumptions or productivity data

## Why this is safe
Individual bid prices are not trade secrets under Idaho law unless we take reasonable steps to keep them secret. By limiting disclosure to the rolled-up actuals and the required subcontractor list, we protect the proprietary information while still giving the owner full visibility into where their money is going.

The owner can request the competing quotes at any time; we provide them under a simple confidentiality acknowledgment so the information is not reused to shop other jobs.
